## Slide 2: The Problem

Accessing clean water is still inconvenient and unreliable for many households and businesses.

- Households may spend a lot of time searching for water vendors or waiting for delivery.
- Customers may not know the correct price, quality, or availability of water.
- Water vendors receive orders through phone calls and WhatsApp, which can lead to missed or confusing orders.
- Restaurants, schools, offices, and families need a reliable water supply.

### Example

A family may run out of drinking water at night but have no easy way to find a nearby supplier who can deliver quickly.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution

MajiLink is a mobile and web platform that connects customers with verified water suppliers.

- Customers can order water refills or deliveries from nearby vendors.
- Vendors can receive and manage customer orders in one place.
- Customers can view prices, delivery estimates, and supplier ratings.
- The platform supports digital payments and order tracking.

### Value Proposition

> Clean water ordered simply, delivered reliably.

---

## Slide 4: Product

MajiLink makes ordering water easy for both customers and vendors.

### Customer Features

- Choose the water type and quantity needed.
- Enter a delivery location.
- Compare available vendors and prices.
- Track the status of an order.
- Pay using M-Pesa or cash on delivery.
- Rate the vendor after delivery.

### Vendor Features

- Receive new customer orders.
- Accept or decline orders.
- Set water prices and available stock.
- Plan delivery routes.
- View customer and sales history.

---

## Slide 5: Target Market

Our first target market is households and small businesses in Garissa Town.

### Target Customers

- Families that frequently purchase drinking water.
- Students and tenants living away from home.
- Restaurants, cafés, and food businesses.
- Small shops and offices.
- Schools and learning institutions.
- Water refill stations and independent water vendors.

### Expansion Plan

MajiLink will start in Garissa Town and later expand to other towns and urban areas in Kenya where water delivery coordination is difficult.

---

## Slide 6: Market Opportunity

Water is an essential and recurring daily need.

- Every household and business requires water regularly.
- Many customers already use mobile phones and M-Pesa for payments.
- Water vendors need better tools to reach customers and manage orders.
- Customers need an easier and more reliable way to order water.
- The business can grow through repeat orders, business subscriptions, and vendor partnerships.

### Initial Opportunity

Our initial focus is customers who already buy water regularly but currently use unreliable methods such as phone calls, WhatsApp messages, or visiting vendors physically.

---

## Slide 7: Business Model

MajiLink earns revenue from each successful water transaction.

### Revenue Streams

- Commission fee charged to vendors for each completed order.
- Small delivery or platform fee paid by customers.
- Monthly premium subscription for vendors.
- Featured listings for vendors who want more visibility.
- Business accounts for schools, offices, and restaurants.

### Example

If a customer places a water order worth KES 500, MajiLink can earn a small commission from the vendor and a platform or delivery fee from the customer.

---

## Slide 8: Competition

Customers currently use informal methods to purchase water, but MajiLink provides a more organized solution.

| Alternative | Limitation | MajiLink Advantage |
|---|---|---|
| Phone calls | Slow and difficult to track | Digital ordering and instant confirmation |
| WhatsApp groups | Unorganized and unreliable | Verified vendors and transparent prices |
| Walking to vendors | Takes time and effort | Water delivery to the customer |
| General delivery apps | Not designed specifically for water | Platform focused on water supply and refills |

### Competitive Advantage

MajiLink focuses on local water delivery, verified suppliers, clear prices, order tracking, and simple digital payments.

---

## Slide 9: Go-To-Market Strategy

MajiLink will begin locally and grow through partnerships, referrals, and digital marketing.

### Customer Acquisition Plan

- Partner with water refill stations and delivery vendors in Garissa.
- Promote the platform through WhatsApp, Facebook, TikTok, and local community groups.
- Offer discounts for first-time customers.
- Give referral rewards to customers who invite friends.
- Visit local businesses, schools, offices, and residential areas.
